1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how TheOneStudio handles information when you use the mobile game SilentAscent (the “Game”).

2. Information handled by the Game

TheOneStudio does not require an account and does not directly request, collect, store, sell, or share personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information.

Game progress, selected character, settings, and earned virtual coins may be stored locally on your device. This local game data is used only to provide gameplay features and is not intentionally transmitted to TheOneStudio.

3. Advertising and third-party processing

The Game displays advertisements supplied by a third-party advertising provider. Although TheOneStudio does not directly collect personal data, an advertising SDK may automatically process or transmit limited device and usage information needed to request, deliver, measure, secure, and prevent fraud in advertisements. Depending on the provider, region, device settings, consent choices, and SDK configuration, this may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a general location;
  • advertising ID, app-set ID, or other device identifiers;
  • app launches, taps, ad impressions, video views, and other interactions;
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as crashes, launch time, and device performance.

This information is processed by the advertising provider under its own terms and privacy policy. If Google Mobile Ads/AdMob is used, see Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

Important: TheOneStudio does not sell this data and does not receive information that directly identifies you from the Game. Third-party SDK processing must still be disclosed in Google Play’s Data safety form.

4. Advertising choices and consent

Where required by law, the Game or its advertising provider may ask for consent before serving personalized ads. You may be able to review or withdraw advertising choices through an in-app privacy message, your device privacy settings, or your Google account ad settings. Non-personalized or limited ads may still use contextual information and limited data for delivery, reporting, security, and fraud prevention.

5. Data sharing

TheOneStudio does not independently share or sell personal information. Limited information may be transmitted directly from the Game to the advertising provider as described above. It may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users, or to maintain the security and integrity of the Game.

6. Data retention and deletion

TheOneStudio does not maintain user accounts or a server-side database of player data. Local game data can be deleted by clearing the Game’s storage or uninstalling the Game. Data processed by an advertising provider is retained and deleted according to that provider’s privacy policy and controls.

7. Security

Because TheOneStudio does not directly collect personal information, it does not maintain a database of such information. We use reasonable safeguards in the Game, and third-party network transmissions should use encrypted connections. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children’s privacy

The Game does not knowingly request personal information from children. Advertising configuration and audience declarations must match the Game’s actual target audience in Google Play Console. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Permissions

The Game may use basic device capabilities such as vibration to provide gameplay feedback. It does not use that capability to collect personal information. Internet access may be used to load advertisements.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game, advertising provider, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use after an update means the revised policy applies.
